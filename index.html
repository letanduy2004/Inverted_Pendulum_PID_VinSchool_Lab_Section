Lyapunov's idea: give the robot a score V, like the "energy" of its mistake. V is 0 only when the stick is perfectly upright and still. If V keeps going down towards 0, the robot is winning.

Challenges

    How does the robot keep its balance?

    1. LookA sensor measures how far the stick is leaning.
    2. ThinkA tiny computer uses P, I and D to work out how hard to push.
    3. PushThe motor turns the arm to catch the stick. Then it looks again…

    The robot repeats these 3 steps 500 times every second. This is called a feedback loop.
